How to use

Estimate how long a conspiracy can stay secret, based on David Grimes' 2016 paper. The more people in on it, the faster it leaks.

  • Press New Conspiracy +, fill in the fields and press Add Conspiracy. Edit and Delete are on each card.
  • The chart draws each theory's chance of having leaked over time. Move the mouse over it to see every theory at that year, or click it and use the arrow keys.
  • Hover over a card to highlight its curve, or press Only this to draw just that theory. Show all brings the rest back.

The controls next to the chart:

  • leak rate: leaks per million people per year. Grimes measured 4.09. This only changes the chart, not the cards.
  • years shown: how many years the chart covers.
  • log time: spreads out the first few years.
  • today dots: mark where each theory is now.
